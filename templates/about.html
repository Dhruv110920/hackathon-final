Our Mission
Why ChainSight exists

Most logistics teams still rely on reactive decision-making — finding out about delays only after they've already happened. We believed that had to change.

ChainSight uses a gradient-boosted XGBoost model trained on real traffic, weather, and logistics data to predict shipment delays up to 48 hours before they occur. By combining live data from four completely free APIs, we deliver enterprise-grade intelligence with zero infrastructure cost.

We believe powerful supply chain tools shouldn't be locked behind expensive enterprise contracts. Open data + thoughtful ML + great UX can compete with anything the market offers — and we built this in 48 hours to prove it.

What ChainSight Does
Six core capabilities in one free platform
ML Delay Prediction
XGBoost model processes 11 real-time features and returns a calibrated delay probability with Low / Moderate / High risk classification in milliseconds.
3-Point Weather Analysis
We fetch live weather at origin, midpoint, and destination simultaneously — then derive worst-case corridor conditions for accurate risk scoring across the full route.
Live Route Mapping
Real driving routes via OpenRouteService, rendered on Leaflet/OSM with traffic-adjusted ETAs and colour-coded risk polylines on a fully interactive map.
Alternative Route Engine
When risk is flagged, ChainSight suggests transport-specific alternative routes, timings, and strategies — visualised live on an interactive modal map with clickable overlays.
Risk Scoring 0–100
Every shipment gets a live probability ring with a single actionable number and colour — no interpretation needed, just see the score and make the call.
Shipment Dashboard
Save unlimited shipments locally and track all active routes in one view — with per-shipment weather, animated probability bars, risk ribbons, and full detail drill-down.

What We Stand For
The principles behind every design decision
01
Radically Open
Every API we use is free. Every model we train is on open data. Powerful tools should never be paywalled from those who need them most.
02
Speed Above All
A prediction that arrives after the delay has happened is useless. We obsess over latency at every layer — from API calls to model inference.
03
Clarity in Complexity
Supply chains are complex. Our job is to make that complexity invisible — one number, one colour, one decision. No jargon, no noise.
04
Built to Grow
We shipped a full ML-powered platform in 48 hours. But we designed every component to scale — from hackathon MVP to enterprise API.
05
Real Impact
We measure success in shipments protected, not features shipped. Every line of code exists to prevent a real logistics failure for a real business.
06
Data-Driven Always
Gut feelings don't run supply chains — data does. Our model is trained on real traffic patterns, real weather events, and real delay outcomes.
